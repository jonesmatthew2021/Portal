Crew list and shift allocation

Complete the Planned Crew list and submit it not later than two weeks before the crew change. Resubmit a new list any time the crew complement changes. For each CC period, select Planned or Actual and the version being submitted. The initial crew list is Planned 1, submitted at least 2 weeks before crew change; any subsequent changes before CC day are Planned 2, and so on. On crew-change day, submit an Actual crew list & shift allocation, and a new crew list for any changes within the crew-change period.

⚠ Less than two weeks’ notice? If you will be changing one or more crew members with less than two weeks’ notice, contact Marina Cooley or Toni Austin directly, as well as completing the required Ad Hoc travel requests and this form.
